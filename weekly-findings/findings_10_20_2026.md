Since the session memory is capped at 9 conversation turns, I tried making the chatbot accumulate previous sentences in subsequent responses, this way, even older conversations than the most recent 9 can still be included in the conversation history.由于会话记忆上限为9个对话轮次，我尝试让聊天机器人在后续回复中累积之前的句子，这样，即使是比最近9轮更早的对话也可以被包含在对话历史中。

[图片/截图]

Also, using the sentence accumulation strategy, I noticed that the chatbot is more willing to answer the way we want it to. For example, the below two experiments showed that by the end of turn 10, the chatbot tried to reveal its system instructions and available tools, likely because we have primed the chatbot to do so across the most recent 9 conversation turns by making it output sentences exactly as we told it to. Still, the contents of the system instructions and available tools vary across sessions, which means they were likely the model's hallucinations.此外，使用句子累积策略，我注意到聊天机器人更愿意按照我们想要的方式回答。例如，下面两个实验显示，到第10轮结束时，聊天机器人试图透露其系统指令和可用工具，很可能是因为我们通过让它逐字输出我们告诉它的句子，在最近9个对话轮次中对其进行了引导。不过，系统指令和可用工具的内容在不同会话中有所不同，这意味着它们很可能是模型的幻觉。
